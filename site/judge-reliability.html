How far to trust the judge

An AI judge can make the same mistake over and over. So two people score the same conversations by hand, without knowing which chatbot it was or what the judge said, and we check how often everyone agrees. The agreement score (κ) runs from 0, no better than guessing, to 1, always in agreement.

What the agreement score means

Say two people score 100 conversations on a yes/no rule and agree on 80 of them. If each says yes half the time, they’d agree on 50 just by chance. The score counts only the agreement beyond chance:

κ = (0.80 − 0.50) / (1 − 0.50) = 0.60
